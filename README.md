\## ✨ Features



\- \*\*10+ powerful tools\*\* — JSON Formatter, Base64, URL Encoder, Color Picker, UUID Generator, Lorem Ipsum, Markdown Previewer, Snippet Manager, Text Counter, Timestamp Converter.

\- \*\*100% client-side\*\* — No backend. No tracking. Your data never leaves your browser.

\- \*\*Beautiful UI\*\* — Modern dark theme with neon accents.

\- \*\*Fully responsive\*\* — Works on desktop, tablet and mobile.

\- \*\*Light \& Dark mode\*\* — Toggle with a single click or `Ctrl + /`.

\- \*\*Favorites \& Recent\*\* — Pin your most-used tools.

\- \*\*Keyboard shortcuts\*\* — `Ctrl + K` to search, `Ctrl + /` to switch themes.

\- \*\*LocalStorage persistence\*\* — Snippets, favorites, and preferences are saved automatically.
